then, the actual day

plan your day on a grid

give it an exact hour, or toss it in a loose morning, afternoon or evening. on a computer, flip from day to week and see all seven.

  • stretchy blocks

    on a computer, pull an edge when the "five minute" thing takes ninety. it snaps to 15 minutes, because 13 is chaos.

  • double-booked? fine.

    overlaps share the hour, fully labelled, no warning badge. an overlap isn't an error, it's a tuesday.

  • a little lime marker

    it shows where you are in today. it does not tap its watch.

  • the planner that never goes red at you.

    no overdue badges, no warning labels, no side-eye. leftovers just show up a little faded.

too much to pick from?

press do stuff. the pile gets moving.

once the braindump gets long, a "do stuff" key shows up in its corner. press it and the pile splits into lanes: do it now, do it for me, today, another day, later, whatever. then the next thing opens in today's free time, with done and skip.

  • fifteen things, all the same weight

    so none of them gets picked. splitting off the quick ones is usually all it takes to start.

  • you're the boss of the lanes

    don't like where something landed? tap its lane chip, or swipe it on a phone.

  • nothing moves until "make it so"

    done counts as you go. the lanes wait for "make it so", then everything lands on its day in one go.

it's on from the start, and it stays out of sight until the list is long enough to need it.

need a hand?

bring your own ai. or don't.

connect openai, anthropic, google gemini, openrouter or anything openai-compatible, or start with google's free key, no card needed. your key is sealed on our server and never shown again, not even the last four.

  • it proposes, you tap

    suggestions arrive as a card. drop any line you don't like, take the rest in one tap, and one undo takes the whole plan back.

  • no ai? still a whole planner

    with no model connected there are no ai buttons, just one "set up ai" key. say "no ai, thanks" and that goes too.

  • dsul never charges for ai

    there's no model key of our own. google's free key costs nothing, other providers bill you directly. when you ask, your question and the bits of your plan it needs go from our server to your model.

  • nothing to paste

    sign in with openrouter in your browser and there's no key to copy at all.

"anything openai-compatible" means a public https address. sorry, localhost. on google's free plan, google may use what you send to improve its products.

way down here, on purpose

teeth, if you want them.

all of it starts off. switch on reminders and they knock at the time you pick, with done and snooze right on the notification. want more? streaks count from day one and the flames show when you say so. there's a last call in the evening, a beeminder goal that puts money on the habit, a friend who gets your day in slack or discord, and a phone that actually rings.

texts and calls run on your own twilio account. in our own words: "a call for every reminder is a lot."
