F · The up-down layout switcher

Linear's capsule, as dsul's layout control. It sits at the end of the capture bar, just above the tabs, and shows the layout you're on. Swipe on it to step through List, Buckets and Schedule; long-press for everything (Day or Week, Go to date). It's our own view inside the bottom accessory rather than a tab, which is why it can take the gestures a tab can't.
